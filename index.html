DeepCTM

A chemistry-aware deep learning surrogate for regulatory chemical transport models. Change the emissions, get a new concentration field.

  • Full atmospheric chemistry
  • Benchmarked against CAMx and CMAQ
  • Runs without an HPC

  • Census-tract resolution
  • Population and demographic data
  • Health outcomes
  • Community health needs assessments
